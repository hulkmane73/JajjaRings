Finding Your Size

Not sure of your ring size? Any of these work:

  • Wrap a strip of paper around your finger, mark the overlap and send us the length in mm.
  • Measure a ring you already wear that fits the same finger.
  • Visit a local jeweler and send us the size they give you.

Caring For Your Ring

  • Remove it before swimming, cleaning or heavy work.
  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth to keep the silver bright.
  • Keep it away from perfume and harsh chemicals.
  • Store it in a pouch so the stone does not scratch.
